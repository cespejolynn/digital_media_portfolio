Glitch

The original images are a collection of previous projects I've made in various digital media classes. The first image was made via audio-bending, the middle was a mixture of audio and data bending, and the final image was data-bending. I had issues with the two pieces that used data-bending, as the files would corrupt easily and not open on my computer. It took a while to get these images produced, but each failure with data-bending helped streamline the process and made image-generation a learning experience for me. In general, I found the audio-bending technique to be more reliable and have more variety when it came to visual outcomes. Each of these images were the result of an entire semester's work and the cause of much stress at one point in time. The original files are perfected and pristine to reflect the grades I wanted out of my work. In glitching them, I am essentially exploring my greatest fear when making anything digitally. One of my digital media professors had a saying, "anything you make online doesn't really exist". He repeated that sentiment and encouraged us to have multiple back-ups of our work throughout the semester.
