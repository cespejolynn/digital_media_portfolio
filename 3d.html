3D

I wanted to do something a little Halloween inspired so I went with an alien abduction scene. I used a mirror modifier to create a symmetrical looking cow and texture painted the features in blender. I also gave the light-source a green tint to evoke an alien feeling. I still wanted some particles in the light beam, so I made a cone and adjusted the opacity and layered the light source on top to create the visual texture. Overall, I would say the hardest part was doing the rendering, as I was going back and forth between how I wanted things to look. After getting used to Blender, I had the most fun making the model and texture painting it. I'm pretty happy the way my piece turned out, but for next time I think I would first create a thumbnail sketch for how I want the scene to look like.
