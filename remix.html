Remix

I combined the spongebob ice cream truck meme with the Willem Dafoe looking up meme because I thought they would match. In the Spongebob meme, he is about to be crushed by the ice cream truck and is panicking. Willem Dafoe is portraying Van Gogh and is looking up at the night sky. Both of the original images are commonly used as reaction images or gifs. Rather than Spongebob panicking to stop his incoming doom, Willem Dafoe can only watch as the ice cream truck barrels down on him.
